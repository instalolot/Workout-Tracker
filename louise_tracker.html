Fiber protocol — for cholesterol
Why fiber matters — more than just cholesterol
Fiber acts like a sponge — it traps cholesterol in your gut and carries it out before it's absorbed, forcing your liver to pull LDL from your blood instead. Studies show 5-10% LDL reduction from fiber alone.

Beyond cholesterol: fiber is one of the strongest dietary predictors of lower colorectal cancer risk (in official cancer prevention guidelines), feeds beneficial gut bacteria, reduces inflammation, and stabilizes blood sugar between meals.
